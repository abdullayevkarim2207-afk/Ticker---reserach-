## Final decision evidence

The integrated SOTP range is $12.80-$33.35 per share, with a $20.47 base,
versus the frozen $12.86 market price at 9:55 a.m. EDT on September 24, 2026.
The committee rule requires at least 15% base-case upside and no low-case
downside. The base clears the first threshold, but the low case is 0.5% below
market, so the action is watch/defer.

The base industrial DCF is not averaged with the approximately $13.78 peer
cross-check. The DCF assumes margin recovery and derives about 79% of base
industrial enterprise value from the terminal value; the peer method
capitalizes depressed current adjusted industrial EBIT at GM's 7.88x multiple.

The reverse DCF indicates that the market price is consistent with a 2026-2030
industrial EBIT-margin path of about 1.46%, 1.96%, 2.46%, 2.76%, and 2.96%,
holding 10% WACC, 3% terminal growth, and Ford Credit at book value.
