# Buffett-style business memo — COST

**Verdict: own the business.**

The filed record shows a business with strong and persistent economics. From the fiscal year ended 2022-08-28 to 2026-08-30, revenue rose from $226,954 million to $303,154 million and net income rose from $5,844 million to $9,226 million.

ROE was 28.3%, 25.1%, 31.2%, 27.8%, and 25.8%. Each result cleared the code's 15% standard. Owner earnings were positive in every displayed year at $3,501 million, $6,745 million, $6,629 million, $7,837 million, and $9,382 million. The final owner-earnings result exceeded the first, so all three code-computed checks pass.

The pattern supports owning the operating business: profitability stayed high while the cash remaining after capital spending increased across the full window. This is a judgment about business quality only. The evidence here contains no price, valuation, moat, or management facts, so it cannot establish whether the shares are attractively priced or whether the qualitative advantages will endure.
