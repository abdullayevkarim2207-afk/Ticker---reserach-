# Ford Motor Company — Lab 04 Evidence and Readiness Summary

## Business and valuation scope

Ford is a global automotive company that earns revenue from gas-powered, hybrid, and electric vehicles; commercial vehicles and related services; and financing through Ford Credit. The FY2024 Form 10-K identifies Ford Blue, Ford Model e, Ford Pro, Ford Next, and Ford Credit as reportable segments.

For an FCFF valuation, the scope must be defined before calculating an enterprise-to-equity bridge. Ford Credit is especially important: its financing receivables, cash, and debt are consolidated with Ford's automotive operations. This summary therefore does **not** calculate a per-share value.

## Main thesis and risks

Ford Pro is the strongest part of Ford's current business. Its commercial trucks, vans, fleet customers, and services produced strong revenue growth and high segment EBIT in 2024. Ford Model e is the main offset: its revenue fell 35% and the segment recorded a $5.076B EBIT loss, driven in part by industry pricing pressure.

Ford's core risks include intense automotive competition and pricing pressure, warranty and recall costs, supplier or supply-chain disruption, macroeconomic conditions, and tariffs. These risks can reduce production, increase costs, or weaken demand and should be tested in a later forecast.

## Reconciled discrepancy and bridge control

The consolidated balance sheet reports $22.935B of cash and cash equivalents at December 31, 2024, while the cash-flow statement reports $23.190B of cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash. The $0.255B difference is caused by restricted cash. In addition, $9.272B of the $22.935B consolidated cash belongs to Ford Credit, leaving $13.663B of cash for the Company-excluding-Ford-Credit disclosure.

The same scope issue applies to debt. Ford reports $20.654B of Company debt excluding Ford Credit, but Ford Credit has $137.868B of debt. Do not subtract Ford Credit debt from a Company-only enterprise value unless Ford Credit's operations and finance receivables are also included consistently.

## Readiness statement

**Ready:** FY2024 operating inputs are sourced to Ford's filed 10-K with dates, units, and arithmetic checks in the evidence ledger.

**Unsafe:** A per-share bridge is unsafe because the valuation date and enterprise value are not established, and the Ford Credit scope has not been reconciled with cash, debt, and operating assets.

**Next test:** Obtain a date-matched enterprise value and diluted share count, then define whether Ford Credit is inside or outside the FCFF and bridge perimeter before calculating equity value.
